How I lead

Listen. Understand. Fix something now. Build the better answer.

I don't believe every problem needs a six-month transformation project. Sometimes the best first move is a small change that makes tomorrow better while the bigger fix is being worked on.

Start with the data. Then talk to the people.

I look at the numbers, but I don't stop there. Frontline teams often know exactly where the friction is—and what needs to change to improve performance.

Make accountability human.

Performance matters. So does understanding why performance looks the way it does. I coach managers, develop people and make expectations clear without losing empathy.

Challenge "the way we've always done it."

I'm an out-of-the-box thinker—but I like solutions that are simple, cost-effective and useful on Monday morning.

Build shared success.

My best work happens when teams, clients and leaders stop protecting their own piece of the puzzle and start solving the problem together.
